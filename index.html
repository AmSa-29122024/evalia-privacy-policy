Privacy Policy for Evalia

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

Overview

Evalia respects your privacy. This extension is designed to run locally in your browser and does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information to external servers.

Data Collection & Usage

We do not collect any user data, browsing history, or personal information. All browser storage (`chrome.storage`), downloads, or tab interactions processed by the extension remain local to your device.

Permissions

The permissions requested by the extension (such as activeTab, downloads, or storage) are strictly used to perform its core single-purpose function on your device.

Third-Party Services

This extension does not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third-party services or analytics providers.
