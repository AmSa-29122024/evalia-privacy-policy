Privacy Policy for Evalia

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

Overview Evalia ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension collects, uses, handles, stores, and shares information when you use our product.

1. Information Collection

To provide core recruitment and candidate evaluation features, we collect the following categories of information:

  • User Account Information: When you register or log in, we collect your email address and credentials. If you choose to log in via your Gmail ID, we collect basic profile authentication identifiers permitted through Google OAuth.
  • Candidate & Professional Data: When you extract or upload data (such as downloading a LinkedIn profile PDF or uploading candidate resumes in bulk), the extension transmits this document data to our secure servers for parsing.
  • Job Descriptions (JDs): Job descriptions you input or upload for comparison against candidate resumes are stored securely on our servers to perform matching analytics.

2. Data Handling and Usage

The information collected is used exclusively to deliver the functionality of the Evalia platform:

  • Account Management: To authenticate your identity, manage your user profile, and secure your access.
  • Resume Parsing & Matching: To process uploaded candidate resumes and LinkedIn profiles, extract text data, and compare them against provided job descriptions to return top-ranking candidate insights.
  • Service Improvement: Data is handled securely to run automated algorithms and AI-driven candidate evaluation pipelines.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Secure Server Storage: User account credentials, parsed resume text, candidate profiles, and job descriptions are stored securely on our cloud servers.
  • Data Isolation: All data uploaded or processed through your account is strictly siloed. We do not share, cross-pollinate, or sell data across different user accounts or profiles.
  • Data Retention: Data remains stored on our servers for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or candidate data to third parties, advertisers, or external data brokers.
  • Third-Party Authentication: When you use Gmail login, authentication is securely handled via Google OAuth protocols. Google shares only authorized profile identifiers necessary for login.
  • Service Providers: We may utilize trusted cloud hosting and database infrastructure providers (such as secure cloud servers) solely to host and operate our backend infrastructure under strict confidentiality standards.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
