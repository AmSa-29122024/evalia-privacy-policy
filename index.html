Privacy Policy for Evalia

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

Overview

Evalia ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension collects, uses, handles, stores, and shares information when you use our product.

1. Information Collection

Evalia is designed with privacy in mind and operates primarily locally on your device.

  • Personal Data: We do not collect, require, or store any personally identifiable information (PII), names, email addresses, or account credentials through the extension.
  • Technical & Usage Data: The extension interacts with browser elements solely to perform its core functions. It processes web page content locally on your active tab only when invoked by you.

2. Data Handling and Usage

Any data processed by Evalia is handled locally within your browser environment:

  • Core Functionality: Information retrieved via permissions (such as activeTab or downloads) is used exclusively on your local device to deliver the single purpose of the extension.
  • No Remote Transmission: Data processed during your session is not transmitted to any external servers, cloud databases, or remote endpoints controlled by us.

3. Data Storage

  • Local Device Storage: The extension may utilize native browser storage mechanisms (such as chrome.storage or local browser cache) strictly to save user preferences or session states locally on your computer.
  • Retention: Data stored locally remains on your device until you clear your browser cache, uninstall the extension, or delete it via the extension settings. We do not maintain server-side backups or historical archives of your data.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer your user data to any external third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.
  • Service Providers: Because no data leaves your device or is collected by us, no data is shared with external vendors or subprocessors.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated effective date on this page.
